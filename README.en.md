## Selected Work

### Legal Advertising Compliance Agent

An AI workspace for law firms reviewing advertising compliance. It connects the work after an AI-generated draft—verification, revision, sign-off, and final delivery—into a complete workflow.

Developed in collaboration with 上海中申律师事务所 (Shanghai Zhongshen Law Firm). The legal team contributes business rules, real review samples, and professional feedback. I handle product design, AI workflows, full-stack development, system integration, evaluation, and deployment.

The central questions: how to make AI judgments traceable to evidence, keep edits within their intended scope, and deliver results that fit real business processes.

[Explore the project ↗](https://furinaaa-cancan.github.io/cancan-portfolio/projects/legal-ai/)

### REM Lab

**Resonance · Embodiment · Musicality**

An independent research group at Glass Bead Academy, founded by Cancan to explore the visual, vocal, and musical expression of virtual characters.

**Live2D · An entirely AI-based creation workflow**

Using Codex to explore illustration, layer separation, modeling, and animation from scratch for livestreaming and creator-led video content. The focus includes character identity, facial expressions, lip sync, motion control, and playful, exaggerated performance.

**AI Voice · Character voices**

Exploring dataset preparation, character speech synthesis, and multilingual expression, with attention to voice consistency, natural pronunciation, and emotional delivery.

**AI Singing · Musical expression**

Connecting vocal separation, voice conversion, phrase-by-phrase refinement, and mixing, then improving the results through comparative listening.

Current work includes a series on Live2D virtual characters, jelly-like eyes, four-corner head-pose synthesis, full hair assembly, and AI singing covers, alongside independent experiments in interactive 3D scenes.

[Explore REM Lab and selected works ↗](https://furinaaa-cancan.github.io/cancan-portfolio/projects/rem-lab/)
